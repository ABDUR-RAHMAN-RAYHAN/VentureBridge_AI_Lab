About VentureBridge

VentureBridge exists because anyone can list a "startup" on the internet, but very few platforms prove that the people and companies behind a listing are who they claim to be. We're building VentureBridge around one idea: trust is the product.

This is the Week 1 milestone: secure accounts, authentication, and role-based dashboards for Founders, Investors, Job Seekers, and Admins. Startup verification, job listings, and the milestone-based investment workflow are built in the phases that follow.
